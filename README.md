### 🏛️ Executive Summary

I am an accomplished **Software Engineer** specializing in **Core Banking Systems (CBS) integration, regulatory compliance automation, and high-concurrency transactional architectures**.

Currently serving as **Programmer at [MicroMac Techno Valley Ltd.](https://micromactvl.com)** (since Dec 2022), I lead the design, development, and production audit of central bank regulatory reporting pipelines, biometrics e-KYC platforms, SWIFT inward/outward remittance processors, and collateral security systems deployed across tier-one banks including **HSBC, Dhaka Bank PLC, City Bank PLC, BRAC Bank PLC, Meghna Bank PLC, NRB Bank PLC**, and **Prime Bank PLC**.

- 🔭 **Currently Building**: High-throughput regulatory ETL bridges and microservices in **Golang (Echo)** and **PHP 8 / Laravel 11**.
- 🛡️ **Core Domain**: **goAML** FIU AML reporting, **BBDAWAR** central bank warehouse pipelines, **CSMS** multi-bank collateral registries, and **ISO 8583 / ISO 20022** payment switches.
- 🏛️ **CBS Engines Connected**: **Infosys Finacle**, **Millennium Ababil** (Islamic Banking), **LEADS BankUltimus**, and **Flora Bank CBS**.
- 📜 **Certified Standards**: Formal audit training in **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** (ISMS) & **ISO 9001:2015** (QMS).
- 🎓 **Education**: B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (CGPA: **3.86 / 4.00**, Southeast University).
